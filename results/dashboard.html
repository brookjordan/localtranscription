Scoring normalises case, punctuation, and timestamps. ok/okay are treated as equivalent, as are non-speech markers ([Music], *whistle*, (laughs)…) regardless of bracket style, and British/American spelling variants (recognise↔recognize, colour↔color…).

Tab key (cards with mitigation tabs, Kyutai only): baseline — greedy decoding of the full clip, model default (temp=0); chunked — the clip cut into blind fixed 30 s windows, resetting decoder state at each boundary; rep-pen — custom sampler penalising the last 64 generated text tokens (−6 logit) to discourage repeats; temp0.6 — stochastic sampling at temperature 0.6 instead of greedy; vad — the clip segmented on Silero-VAD speech boundaries (silence cuts, ≤30 s of speech per group). Chunked/rep-pen/temp0.6/vad tabs are mitigation strategies applied on top of baseline — context and findings: kyutai-mitigations.md.
