What the transcripts actually say

2026-09-08 12:00 · resultscomparison

A comparison is only useful if the transcripts remain visible instead of being reduced to a single winner badge.


Audio: conrad.wav

As soon as it was early morning, the chief priests, elders, and teachers of the law, and the whole high court, had Jesus tied up and then handed him over to the Roman governor Pilate.

On the Conrad reading sample, Parakeet, Moonshine, Whisper variants, Qwen3, and the large Whisper models mostly agreed on the sentence while differing around “teachers of the law”, punctuation, and “Pilate”. SenseVoice was visibly rougher. Kyutai did not produce a transcript because it did not load.

The useful bit

The dashboard therefore keeps the text, timing, engine, and notes together. A single accuracy number is useful for sorting; it is not a substitute for reading what the model heard.
