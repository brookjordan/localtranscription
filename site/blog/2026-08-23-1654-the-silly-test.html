A benchmark needs speech that is easy to recognise and speech that is not trying very hard to be sensible.

Yeah boy, I think that would be so cool. I go in the grass with the bath and I laugh at you. Ha ha ha.

The second clip was intentionally ridiculous. The words were clear enough to be a test, but strange enough to expose whether a model was listening or merely producing plausible English.

The useful bit

The tiny-en result preserved the joke well enough. Later engines disagreed on punctuation more than on the basic words. That distinction matters: a transcript can be useful while still being visibly imperfect.
