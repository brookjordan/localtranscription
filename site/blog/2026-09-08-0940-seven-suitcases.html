The corpus grew from three personal clips into a mixed set of speech, accents, voices, long-form reading, noise, and singing. The point was not to crown one model from one sentence. It was to see where each one stopped being dependable.

The useful bit

On the short smoke samples, the fast models were genuinely fast: Qwen3 and Whisper-family runs commonly landed around 0.03–0.13 RTF in the recorded JSON results, while the heavier baseline runner was around 2–4 RTF. Those are not interchangeable quality scores, but they are useful operating numbers.
