VAD is a lever, not a detail

2026-09-08 10:10 · lessonvad

The Kyutai experiments made one thing obvious: segmentation is part of transcription quality.


Audio: brook.wav

Okay then, let me do a quick test and see how this ends up sounding. I'm not quite sure what I'm going to say, so I make it up and I'll transcribe it later.

Silero-VAD boundaries improved some of the short personal clips dramatically. The recorded Kyutai VAD results include 94.9% for `brook`, 92.0% for `silly`, and 96.7% for the lockpicking-lawyer sample, while other voices collapsed almost completely.

The useful bit

That spread is the lesson. VAD is not a magic quality switch. It is a lever whose setting interacts with speaker, silence, noise, and model. Keep the boundary configuration in the result note, or the number has no useful meaning.
