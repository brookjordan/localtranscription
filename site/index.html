localtranscription

A small, isolated speech-to-text evaluation harness for Apple Silicon. Local audio in, inspectable transcripts out.

What it tests

Whisper.cpp, Whisper variants, SenseVoice, Parakeet, Moonshine, Qwen3-ASR, and Kyutai against an explicit local corpus.

What it is not

Not a server, not a Telegram integration, and not a production claim disguised as a demo.
